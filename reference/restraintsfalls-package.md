# restraintsfalls: Physical Restraint Use and Falls in Nursing Homes

Research compendium for the analysis of the association between physical
restraint use and falls in nursing homes in the province of Trento,
Italy. Uses longitudinal quality monitoring data from the Indicare
Salute Lab system (2016-2025).
